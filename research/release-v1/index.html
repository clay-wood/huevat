Huevat

Colors for scientific figures.

Palettes and gradients for Matplotlib, Plotly and UltraPlot, with a figure doctor for coordinated colors, markers and dashes.

Clearer is the default categorical palette. Choose light or dark variants to suit your figure. The eighteen additional named gradients are supported alternatives.

import huevat as hv
palette = hv.categorical(6)
gradient = hv.diverging()
hv.export(palette, labels=["Control", "A", "B", "C", "D", "E"]).write("palette.json")
restored = hv.load_export(open("palette.json", encoding="utf-8").read()).design
