Methods and limits

Every shipped variant passes unchanged normal/CVD float/8-bit Oklab and CAM02-UCS profiles, complete-segment numerical checks and the denser ordering audit. Sampling counts are screened individually; uniform and optimized sampling remain distinct. A plotted view, shaded composite or domain label has no universal accessibility or scientific-validity guarantee.
