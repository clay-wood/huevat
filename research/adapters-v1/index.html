Nine colors with a neutral midpoint

The default g.discrete() selection uses nine colors and includes the exact neutral midpoint. The central data bin spans −0.1 to +0.1, so zero is represented by the neutral color. Physical data boundaries are chosen separately from color sample positions. Explicit optimized eight-color selections remain in the linked numerical evidence.
